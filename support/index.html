Common questions

Does Ayath work without the internet?

Yes. Reading, prayer times, Qibla, duas, Tasbih and Hifz Coach checks work offline. Ayath goes online only when you stream or save a recitation, download the offline speech model the first time you turn on Hifz Coach or Recite to find, or buy Pro through Google Play.

Is my voice recorded?

No. Hifz Coach and Recite to find compare your recitation with the Quran text using a speech model on your phone. Your voice is held in memory only for that check. It is not saved, uploaded or shared, including with us.

Hifz Coach marked a word I recited correctly.

Hifz Coach is a reading aid, not a judgement of tajweed, and it can make mistakes, especially with background noise. Recite clearly in a quiet place, close to the phone. Your teacher and the mushaf remain the authority.

My prayer times look wrong.

Open the prayer settings and check the calculation method and the Asr method. Different regions follow different methods. Then check that the location is right: use your device location, or enter your location by hand in the same settings.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you bought Pro with, open the Pro screen in Ayath and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is billed by Google Play. Open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Ayath and cancel. The Pro screen in Ayath also links there. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

I can't afford Pro.

If the price is hard for you right now, whether you are a student of hifz, a teacher or going through a difficult time, write to us and we will send a free code. No proof needed.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Ayath remembers is stored on your phone. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it, and it is gone. We have no copy to delete. If you turned on the optional backup to your own Google Drive, delete that copy from the app's Backup & restore screen or from your Google Account.

I found a mistake in the Quran text or a translation.

Please email us the surah, ayah and what you saw. Ayath uses openly published sources with recorded provenance and never edits sacred text itself, so we check the report against the source before anything changes.
