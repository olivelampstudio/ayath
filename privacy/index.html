Privacy policy

Your reading and your voice stay with you.

Effective date: 17 October 2026. Wherever this says "we" or "the app", that means Olivelamp Studio, the developer of Ayath.

Short version

Ayath's core reading and prayer features work offline. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. Prayer settings, Tasbih counts, Khatam progress, Zakat estimates and downloaded content are stored on your device. Optional online recitation sends a recording request to MP3Quran and its audio CDN; those providers receive the network request, including your IP address, requested recording and request time. Optional Google Drive backup and Google Play purchases use Google's services as described below. Ayath stores your Pro entitlement locally.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Location (approximate). Used only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. Resolved in memory each time it's needed; not stored with any identifier, not sent anywhere.
  • Microphone. Used only when you start "Recite to find" or a Hifz Coach check. The app listens to your recitation and compares it with the Quran text using a speech model that runs on your device. Your voice is held in memory only for that check: it is never saved as a recording, never uploaded, never used to train any model, and never shared with anyone, including us. Hifz Coach keeps only the result you see (for example, which words to check again) on your device.
  • Notifications. If you turn them on, the app can show prayer-time reminders, an optional lock-screen prayer notification, a daily Dua/Hadith notification, a reading reminder after a prayer you choose, and a reminder before a Pro free trial ends. All are scheduled and shown entirely on your device.
  • Internet access. The app is offline-first. It only reaches the internet for a few things, all of which you start yourself or which are a normal part of a purchase you make: streaming a recitation you select or saving an individual surah from MP3Quran and its approved audio CDN (no automatic full-pack download); downloading the optional offline speech model used by "Recite to find" and Hifz Coach, once, the first time you turn either on, from the model's public release on GitHub (which receives the network request, including your IP address and request time); and, if you buy Ayath Pro, Google Play Billing talking to Google's own servers to show prices and process the purchase (see "Ayath Pro purchases" below). Recitation providers receive your IP address, requested recording and request time; see MP3Quran's privacy and permission notice. The speech model is checked against its pinned checksum. Recitation downloads preserve the original bytes and receive a local SHA-256 digest, checked before offline playback. This detects changes to the saved file; it is not a publisher-supplied checksum or recording certification. Recitation requests include no prayer history, notes, microphone recordings or reading progress.
  • Optional backup, only if you turn it on. Backup & restore (in Me and in Settings → Content & data) lets you sign in with your own Google account so the app can save a copy of your progress — reading positions, bookmarks, notes, plans, Tasbih/tracking/Zakat/recitation history, preferences, widget styles and notification toggles — to your own Google Drive. It never includes Quran/Hadith/Dua text itself, downloaded content packs or audio, or anything specific to your device. This is opt-in and never required; the onboarding card offering it has a clear Skip. The backup goes into a hidden, app-only folder in your Drive (Google's drive.appdata scope) — it does not appear in your regular Drive files, and no other app can see it. We do not receive it. There is no server of ours involved anywhere in this feature; Google is your own storage provider, acting on your account under its own terms. Google Sign-In is used solely to authorize this backup — it does not create an account with us. Restoring checks a version number and checksum first and refuses a file that fails that check; the app also keeps one automatic local copy of what a restore is about to replace, on your own device, so "Undo last restore" can put it back. Turn off auto-backup, delete the Drive copy, or sign out any time from the Backup & restore screen.

Ayath Pro purchases, handled by Google Play

Ayath Pro (monthly or yearly, with a free trial when Google Play offers you one; the Pro screen shows its length and the price before you start) is entirely optional and is sold through Google Play's own billing system, not by us directly:

  • When you tap to buy, Google Play shows you the price, collects your payment method and processes the transaction. Ayath never sees or stores your card number, bank details or any other payment information — that entire exchange happens between you and Google.
  • What Ayath itself stores, only on your device, is the fact that a purchase exists: which product you bought (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and the date it was last confirmed with Google Play. This is what lets Pro keep working for up to 7 days if you briefly lose your internet connection, without asking Google every single time the app opens.
  • Ayath asks Google Play, not a server of ours, whether your purchase is still valid. There is no purchase database of ours anywhere — Google Play is the sole record of what you bought, and you can view or manage your subscription any time from Google Play's own "Subscriptions" page (also linked from inside the app's Pro screen).
  • Google Play's billing library, which Ayath includes so you can buy Pro, sends Google diagnostic information about its own operation (for example, whether a billing request succeeded or a connection failed), which Google uses to keep its billing service working. This is handled under Google's own privacy policy; Ayath does not receive it.
  • Restore purchases (also on the Pro screen) simply asks Google Play again which of your purchases apply to this install — useful after reinstalling the app or switching devices signed into the same Google account. It does not involve us.
  • Cancelling, refunds and payment disputes are handled entirely through Google Play's own subscription management and support, per Google's own terms — Ayath has no separate billing relationship with you to cancel.

Sharing, feedback and the optional survey

  • Share links. When you share Ayath or a card from it, the Google Play link carries a few fixed words naming where it was shared from (for example utm_campaign=card_ayah). Google Play uses them to count visits and installs by link. They contain nothing about you.
  • Feedback and the optional survey. "Feedback & support" and, about two weeks after installing, an optional three-question survey in Me let you write to us. Your answers stay on your device until you tap Send, which opens a draft in your own email app; nothing is sent unless you send that email. If you do, we receive what you wrote and your email address, and use them only to reply and to improve Ayath.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind is required to use the app, including Ayath Pro. The only sign-in anywhere in the app is the optional Google Sign-In described above, used solely to authorize backup to your own Drive — it does not create an account with us, and buying Pro never requires it.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind are built into the app. The only diagnostics that leave your phone are those Google Play's billing library sends to Google about its own operation (see "Ayath Pro purchases" above).
  • No personal information (name, email, contacts, etc.) is collected by the app, because it never asks for it. If you choose to email us from your own email app, we receive that email (see above). Google Play, not Ayath, collects whatever it needs to process a Pro purchase, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • No data is sold or rented. User-requested recitation, backup and purchases communicate with the providers described above; recitation requests do not include your local progress, notes or microphone audio.
  • No automatic, silent backup of app data to any cloud account: the app opts out of Android's own OS-level auto-backup. The only cloud backup that ever happens is the optional, off-by-default one described above, and only after you sign in yourself.

Data storage and deletion

Everything the app remembers (settings, Tasbih/Khatam progress, downloaded packs, and — if you bought Pro — which product and when it was last confirmed) is stored locally on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it — there is no server-side copy of ours to separately delete. If you turned on the optional Google Drive backup, that copy lives only in your own Drive; delete it any time with "Delete cloud backup" in the app's Backup & restore screen, or from your own Google Account. Your Google Play purchase history lives in your Google account and is managed there, independent of the app.

Religious source text

Quran text, translations, Hadith and Dua text bundled with the app come from permission-cleared sources with recorded provenance (see the in-app "About & Credits" screen). This policy covers your privacy, not licensing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal data from any user. Ayath Pro purchases require a Google Play account capable of making purchases, governed by Google Play's own age and payment policies.
