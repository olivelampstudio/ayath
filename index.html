Questions

Is Ayath free?

Yes. The whole Quran and every translation, prayer times and reminders, Qibla, duas, hadith, Tasbih, your revision plan, every Juz 'Amma Hifz Coach check and one of every widget are free. Ayath Pro is optional.

Does Ayath work offline?

Yes. Reading, prayer times, Qibla, duas and Hifz Coach checks work without the internet. Ayath goes online only when you stream or save a recitation, download the speech model once, or buy Pro through Google Play.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

No. Hifz Coach compares your recitation with the Quran text using a speech model on your phone. Your voice is held in memory only for that check and is never saved, uploaded or shared.

Does Hifz Coach check tajweed?

No. It is a reading aid that shows which words to check again. Your teacher and the mushaf remain the authority.
